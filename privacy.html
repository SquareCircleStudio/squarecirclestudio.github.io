What this app is

SquareCircle Publisher is a private tool used only by SquareCircleStudio to publish its own short videos to its own accounts on YouTube, TikTok and Instagram, and to read the view statistics of those videos. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

What data we access

  • Basic profile information of our own connected accounts (account name, channel or profile ID).
  • Our own videos and their public statistics (views, likes, comments, shares).
  • Permission to upload videos to our own accounts.

We do not access, collect or store data of other users, viewers or followers.

How we use and store data

Statistics are stored on our own computer and home server to show them on a private dashboard. Access tokens are stored locally and never shared. We do not sell, rent or share any data with third parties and do not use it for advertising.

Google / YouTube

This app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you are also bound by the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. You can revoke the app's access at any time via Google security settings. The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

TikTok and Instagram

Use of TikTok and Instagram is also subject to the TikTok and Meta (Instagram) terms and privacy policies. Access can be revoked at any time in the security settings of those apps.

Data deletion

All stored tokens and statistics can be deleted on request by emailing the contact address above; we delete them within 30 days.
